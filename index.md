# Privacy Policy: Market-Ready Taxonomy (Cyjna)

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Market-Ready Taxonomy is an internal, read-only tool operated by Cyjna for its own design research.

**What we access.** With the account owner's permission, we read boards and pins from the owner's own Pinterest account only (scopes: boards:read, pins:read). We do not access any other user's data.

**What we store.** For each pin we store only its pin ID, board name, destination link and tags. We do not store or redistribute Pinterest images or other users' content.

**What we don't do.** We do not post, edit or delete pins. We do not run ads, sell or share data with third parties, or use Pinterest content to train AI or machine-learning models.

**Retention and deletion.** Stored data is kept only while the tool is in use and is deleted on request or when access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time from Pinterest's account settings.

**Contact.** Questions can be raised via https://cyjna.com.
